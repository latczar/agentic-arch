# Getting a property onto the market

**When this applies.** A property is ready to advertise and has to appear in the
places buyers or tenants look.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Gather the details, photographs, floorplan and energy rating.
2. Write the description.
3. Set the price and any terms.
4. Get the owner to approve how it appears.
5. Publish to the website and the portals.
6. Check it actually arrived and looks right on each one.
7. Keep it in step when anything changes.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Owner approval before it goes live.** It is their property and their price,
  and a listing published without sign off is awkward to unpublish.
- **The description, if it makes claims.** Anything about condition, dimensions
  or what is included is a statement somebody may rely on.

## What usually goes unasked

- Who signs off the listing, and does silence count as approval?
- What happens when a portal rejects it?
- Does a price change need approving again?

## Worth saying plainly

Publishing is external and public, so it belongs in the group that gets a pair
of eyes. Keeping an already approved listing in step across portals afterwards
is much safer ground.
