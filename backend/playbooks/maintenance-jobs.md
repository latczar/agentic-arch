# Maintenance report through to contractor and invoice

**When this applies.** A tenant reports something broken and it ends with a job
done and paid for.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Take the report, by phone, email or a form.
2. Record it against the property and the tenancy.
3. Work out how urgent it is.
4. Decide whether the landlord has to authorise the spend.
5. Instruct a contractor.
6. Track it to completion and confirm with the tenant.
7. Take the invoice and pay it, usually from the landlord's money.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Judging urgency.** No heating in January and a dripping tap are both
  "plumbing", and only one of them is same day.
- **Spending the landlord's money above their limit.** It is somebody else's
  money, which is the whole reason the limit exists.
- **Anything raising a safety question.** Gas, electrics and anything about to
  hurt somebody stop being a workflow question.

## What usually goes unasked

- What is the landlord's authorisation limit, and is it per job or per month?
- Who decides urgency, and against what definition?
- What happens if the contractor does not respond?
