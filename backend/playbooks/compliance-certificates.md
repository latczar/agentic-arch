# Safety certificate renewals

**When this applies.** Properties carry certificates that expire, and somebody
has to make sure they are renewed before they do.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Find what is expiring, usually on a look ahead of a month or two.
2. Check whether it has already been booked.
3. Tell the landlord it is due, and what it costs.
4. Book an engineer or inspector.
5. Arrange access with the tenant.
6. Collect the certificate and file it.
7. Send a copy where it is required to go.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Anything that has lapsed or is about to.** An expired safety certificate is
  a legal problem, not a diary problem, and it stops being routine.
- **Booking access.** It is somebody's home and their availability decides it.

## What usually goes unasked

- How far ahead should the reminder run?
- Who pays, and does the landlord have to agree each time?
- What happens if the tenant will not give access?

## Worth saying plainly

The reminder and the booking are ordinary work. The consequence of missing one
is not ordinary, so the escalation matters more here than the automation does.
