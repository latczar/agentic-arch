# Supplier invoice processing and payment

**When this applies.** Invoices arrive from people you owe money to, get recorded
somewhere, and eventually get paid.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Collect invoices as they arrive, usually by email, usually as attachments.
2. Read the amount, the supplier and the reference off each one.
3. Check it against whatever authorised the work.
4. Record it in the ledger or spreadsheet.
5. Approve it, if it is over whatever threshold needs approving.
6. Pay it.
7. Mark it paid and file the paperwork.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **The payment itself.** Money leaving an account is the step that cannot be
  taken back, and the one everybody regrets automating first.
- **Approval above a threshold.** Most places have a figure above which somebody
  senior looks. Below it, the check is the ledger.
- **Anything that does not match.** An invoice for work nobody remembers
  ordering is exactly the case worth a person's attention.

## What usually goes unasked

- Is there an approval limit, and what is the figure?
- What happens to an invoice that does not match a purchase order?
- Are duplicates caught, and on what: reference, amount, or both?
