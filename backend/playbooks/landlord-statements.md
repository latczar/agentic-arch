# Landlord statement and payout

**When this applies.** Rent has been collected, costs have come out of it, and
what is left goes to the landlord with a statement explaining it.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Work out what came in for each landlord in the period.
2. Work out what comes off: commission, works, any retention.
3. Produce the statement.
4. Check the figures and that the account balances.
5. Pay the landlord.
6. Send the statement, with the invoices behind it.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **The payment.** Client money, somebody else's, and irreversible.
- **Anything that does not balance.** A statement that nearly reconciles is the
  one worth stopping on, and the arithmetic being confident is not the same as
  it being right.
- **The first statement for a new landlord.** The one most likely to reveal the
  setup was wrong.

## What usually goes unasked

- What is the payment run cycle, and what happens to money arriving just after?
- Are works costs retained against future rent, or invoiced separately?
- Who checks the reconciliation before anything is sent?
