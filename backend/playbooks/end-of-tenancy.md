# End of tenancy, check-out and deposit return

**When this applies.** A tenancy is ending and the deposit has to be settled.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Confirm the end date and arrange the check-out.
2. Inspect, against the inventory taken at the start.
3. Note what has changed and what is fair wear.
4. Price anything being proposed as a deduction.
5. Put it to the tenant.
6. Agree it, or go to the scheme if it is disputed.
7. Return the balance and close the tenancy off.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Deciding what is wear and what is damage.** Genuinely a judgement, argued
  over constantly, and no rule covers it.
- **Proposing a deduction.** It is money belonging to somebody else and the
  proposal is the thing they may dispute.
- **Returning the deposit.** Money out, protected by a scheme, with timescales.

## What usually goes unasked

- Who prices the deductions, and against what evidence?
- What is the timescale, and what starts the clock?
- What happens when the tenant does not respond?

## Worth saying plainly

Almost every step here touches somebody else's money and their record. The
scheduling and reminders automate. The settlement does not.
