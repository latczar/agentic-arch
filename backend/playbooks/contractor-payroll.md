# Contractor and staff payment run

**When this applies.** People who work for you get paid on a cycle, with a
document showing what they were paid and why.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Collect what each person is owed: hours, commission, fixed pay, expenses.
2. Work out deductions.
3. Produce a payslip or remittance for each person.
4. Get the run approved.
5. Pay everybody.
6. Send each person their document.
7. File the run and post it to the accounts.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Approving the run before it goes.** The last point at which a wrong figure
  costs a correction rather than a recovery.
- **The payment.** Money out, irreversible, and to people who notice.
- **Sending payslips.** They contain personal and financial information about an
  identifiable person, so a payslip sent to the wrong address is a data breach
  rather than an inconvenience.

## What usually goes unasked

- Who approves the run, and what do they actually check?
- What happens to a correction found after payment?
- How are the documents delivered, and is that channel private enough?
