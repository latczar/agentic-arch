# New enquiry through to booked viewing

**When this applies.** Enquiries arrive from portals, the website or the phone,
and somebody turns them into viewings.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Collect the enquiry, from a portal feed, an inbox or a form.
2. Record the applicant and what they are looking for.
3. Check whether they are already known.
4. Match them against available properties.
5. Get back to them, usually fast, because speed is most of the win.
6. Offer times and book one.
7. Confirm it with the applicant and whoever holds the keys.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Qualifying the enquiry.** Budget, timing and whether they are actually able
  to proceed are judgement, and getting it wrong wastes a viewing slot.
- **The first reply, if it is not a simple acknowledgement.** Anything that
  reads as a commitment on price or availability is a person's to send.

## What usually goes unasked

- Is an instant acknowledgement acceptable, or must the first reply be personal?
- What happens outside office hours?
- How is a duplicate applicant spotted, and what happens then?
