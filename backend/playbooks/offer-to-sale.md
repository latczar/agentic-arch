# Offer through to memorandum of sale

**When this applies.** Somebody offers on a property and it ends with the sale
agreed and the paperwork issued.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Take the offer and record it.
2. Check the buyer's position and how they are funding it.
3. Put it to the seller.
4. Handle the back and forth.
5. Record the accepted figure and any conditions.
6. Issue the memorandum to both sides and the solicitors.
7. Update the listing status.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Everything up to acceptance.** Negotiating is the job, and nothing in it is
  a rule you can write down.
- **Issuing the memorandum.** It goes to four parties, it states terms and
  people act on it immediately.
- **Verifying the buyer's funding.** A judgement on evidence, and the reason
  chains fall through.

## What usually goes unasked

- Are all offers put to the seller, including ones below a stated minimum?
- How quickly must an offer be acknowledged?
- What conditions get recorded, and who decides they are met?

## Worth saying plainly

This is the least automatable process in the set. Recording and notifying are
fine. The decisions are the work.
