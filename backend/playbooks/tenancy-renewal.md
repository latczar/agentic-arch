# Tenancy renewal and rent review

**When this applies.** A fixed term is coming to an end and somebody decides
what happens next.

## The shape it usually takes

1. Find the tenancies ending in the next few months.
2. Ask the landlord what they want to do.
3. Decide whether the rent changes, and by how much.
4. Put it to the tenant.
5. Handle the reply, which is often a negotiation.
6. Draw up and sign the new agreement.
7. Update the records, the standing order and any protection registration.

## Where a person is usually needed

- **Setting the new rent.** It is a judgement about the market, the property and
  a tenant you would rather keep, and a number that is wrong in either direction
  costs real money.
- **Anything sent to the tenant about price.** It is external, it is about
  money, and it is quoted back at you later.
- **Signing.** An agreement binds both sides, so the signature is the point of
  the step.

## What usually goes unasked

- How far ahead does the renewal conversation start?
- Who decides the rent, the landlord or the agent?
- What happens if nobody replies and the term simply ends?
